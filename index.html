AI safety research Oct – Nov 2026

Catching AI sabotage on a budget

BlueDot Impact · Technical AI Safety Project

More and more code is written by AI, and a model could hide a backdoor in it. A common defence is a monitor, a second AI that reads each program and flags the suspicious ones for a human to check. Smaller labs and businesses have no good guide to which monitor is worth paying for.

With Anusha Asim and Marnick Vandecauter, I tested 13 monitors on 500 coding problems with backdoors hidden in them, and priced every run. When a human could check 2% of the code, Sonnet 5.5 caught 88% of the backdoors and Haiku 5.5 caught 85% for a fifth of the price. If a human can only check 1% or less, Sonnet's lead grows to between 12 and 27 points.

  • More reasoning raised catch rates for 6 of 8 models, by up to 28 points
  • Sent a fix to ControlArena, the UK AI Security Institute's control framework
