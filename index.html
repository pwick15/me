AI safety research Since Oct 2026

AI control that labs can say yes to

BlueDot Impact · Technical AI Safety Project

AI labs can have a trusted model watch their most capable one and step in when something looks off. Few do it well, and cost is why: at frontier scale, even 1% overhead runs into billions.

We're running the first like-for-like test of AI control methods: the same attackers and the same models for every method, scored on safety, usefulness and dollars. The result is a map of the best protection money can buy. Next, we take on the open problem of what to do once a model gets caught.

About
